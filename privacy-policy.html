Zoobloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Zoobloom: Idle Zoo Park 3D ("Zoobloom", "the game", "we") collects, and how it is used. Zoobloom is made by Zoobloom Games, an independent developer.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional reward videos from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads.
  • Anonymous play statistics (your level and similar) may be sent with a random ID, never your name. You can switch them off in Settings.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Zoobloom saves your game progress and settings (for example your zoo, level, coins, gems and sound settings) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Zoobloom offers optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch an ad to get an in-game reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address
  • information about your device (model, operating system, language)
  • your interactions with ads (views and taps)

Google may use this information to show personalised ads, depending on your settings and where you live. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings > Google > Ads (on some phones: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy gems and coins in Zoobloom. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made (a purchase token and the item bought) so the game can deliver it. We never receive your name, card number or billing address.

Anonymous play statistics (GameAnalytics)

To find levels that are too hard or too slow, the game can send anonymous play statistics to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark). These include the level you reached, which zoo areas you opened, how long a play session lasted, the game version and basic device information (phone model, Android version). Each install gets a random ID made on your phone. It is not your advertising ID and it is not linked to your name, email or Google account. Statistics are only sent after Google's consent message for ads (where it is shown) has been answered, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings > "Share anonymous play statistics". GameAnalytics' privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy

Crash and performance data

Google Play may collect anonymous crash reports and performance data (Android vitals) to help us fix problems. This data does not identify you.

Children

Zoobloom is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data about them was collected through ads, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without ever watching an ad.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.
  • You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best sent to Google, but you can always contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address.
